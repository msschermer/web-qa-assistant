    Writing

    Lumen composes its own findings, order, counts and confidence. A model is only ever asked to write sentences and to draft replacement text, and a reply that changes a number, an order or a confidence level is rejected.

    What is sent: the discipline names, rule ids, severity, confidence and counts of the ranked areas. Never a URL, a page title, page markup or the site's name. The request goes from this browser to your endpoint; it does not pass through Lumen's servers.
